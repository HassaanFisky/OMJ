# OMJ — SCENE BIBLE & CINEMATIC STORYBOARD

## Scene Registry (Scenes A through Q + Original Motifs)

### Scene A: The Quiet Beginning (Night Interior & Window)
- **Framing:** Rule of thirds; male teddy positioned screen-left, angled toward screen-right window.
- **Environment:** Cozy candlelit room, moonbeams slicing through sheer curtains, distant twinkling stars.
- **Emotion:** Solitude, anticipation, gentle breathing idle animation.

### Scene B: The Arrival
- **Framing:** Medium shot; female teddy enters from screen-right with gentle footfalls.
- **Action:** She reaches down, offers her plush paw, and helps him stand; soft mutual blush blooms.
- **Atmospheric Detail:** Subtle ambient light pulse signaling arrival.

### Scene C: The Two-Character World
- **Framing:** Close-up two-shot with shallow depth of field.
- **Action:** Innocent, adorable plush kiss. Natural head tilt, eyes softly close, noses touch with subtle squish deformation.

### Scene D: The Separation
- **Framing:** Wide shot; female teddy slowly retreats into misty background along the z-axis.
- **Emotion:** Male teddy watches helplessly; eyes glisten, a single crystal tear forms and falls.

### Scene E: The Night Sky
- **Framing:** Low-angle camera looking up past male teddy lying on a soft grassy mound toward a vast starry sky.
- **Visuals:** Cosmic nebulas in deep indigo and violet, drifting stardust particles.

### Scene F: Rain and Light
- **Framing:** Over-the-shoulder view through glass.
- **Visuals:** Interactive Canvas raindrops striking glass, running down with gravity physics; golden sunbeams slowly pierce gray storm clouds.

### Scene G: Nervous Affection
- **Framing:** Two-shot with awkward spatial tension.
- **Action:** Male teddy fidgets, shifts paws behind back, glances down shyly; female teddy smiles warmly in return.

### Scene H: The Gifts
- **Framing:** Centered presentation; male teddy reveals handcrafted gift boxes tied with silk ribbons.
- **Action:** Floating sparkle particles erupt gently as each ribbon unravels.

### Scene I: The Heart Environment
- **Framing:** Cinematic multiplane parallax.
- **Visuals:** Depth-layered floating heart particles drifting upward and sideways with varying velocities, blur, and opacity.

### Scene J: The Winter Transformation
- **Framing:** Window view transitioning into icy frost fractals creeping along glass borders; soft crystalline snowflakes descending.

### Scene K: The Distraction
- **Framing:** Contrast shot; female teddy engrossed in an illuminated handheld device, male teddy looking on with longing and gentle patience.

### Scene L: The Imagination Cloud
- **Framing:** Dream vignette; a fluffy thought cloud expands overhead, revealing a warm sunlit dreamscape where both teddies dance and embrace. Dissolves back to reality smoothly.

### Scene M: The Comfort Moment
- **Framing:** Extreme close-up emotional anchor.
- **Action:** Male teddy rests his head tenderly in female teddy's lap; she strokes his plush ears, wiping away sorrow.

### Scene N: Reconnection
- **Framing:** Golden hour two-shot; characters step closer into a full, deep embrace. Warm rim lighting floods the frame.

### Scene O: The Decorated Window
- **Framing:** Festive interior window lined with fairy lights, paper snowflakes, and warm draped curtains. Both teddies sit shoulder to shoulder.

### Scene P: The Shared Blanket
- **Framing:** Intimate medium shot; both teddies snuggled under a heavy knit quilt with realistic fabric folds, steam rising from cups nearby as blizzard blows outside.

### Scene Q: The Final World
- **Framing:** Extreme wide pull-back shot; camera glides back into the stars as the warm window becomes a gentle glowing ember in the cosmos. Fades to the OMJ emblem.

## Original Creative Motifs
1. **The Firefly Guide:** A single warm luminescent ember that flutters across scene cuts, guiding the viewer's focal eye.
2. **The Foggy Window Heart:** Teddy draws a small heart outline on misted glass, revealing the sky outside.
3. **The Puddle Reflection:** Rain puddles rippling to reveal the next emotional phase.
4. **The Snow Globe:** A miniature winter world inside a glass sphere that expands into the full screen environment.
5. **The Shared Scarf:** One teddy wraps their warm wool scarf around the other's neck in falling snow.
